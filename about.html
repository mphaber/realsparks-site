About Real Sparks

Real Sparks LLC is a design, marketing, and consulting studio founded in 2011. It was founded on a simple idea: excellent service and accessible technology for businesses that do not have time for either to be complicated. Its very first project was the website for Volunteer Realty in Knoxville, Tennessee, and Real Sparks still manages their email today.

These days Real Sparks works the same way it always has: quietly with small businesses that want a website that loads fast, says what they do, and stays out of their way. Alongside the client work, Real Sparks now also designs and publishes small, calm browser games for families, which live at games.realsparks.net.

About Matthew Haber

Matthew Haber is the owner of Real Sparks and has worked in website design and marketing for 30 years. Before and alongside Real Sparks, his career ran through luxury real estate marketing, digital consulting, marketing technology, and automotive software, including serving as Executive Vice President for Luxury Real Estate and as Director of Interactive Marketing for a large regional brokerage.

In his day job, Matthew is a Principal Marketing Technologist at Zillow, where he helps define the strategy behind one of the industry's largest marketing technology ecosystems, working at the intersection of marketing, engineering, finance, and strategy. Real Sparks is his independent studio: the client work that started it, and now the games too.

Real Sparks is based in Norwell, Massachusetts, where Matthew works from home.
