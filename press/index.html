Bios, the games list, and contact details for Real Sparks and its owner, Matthew Haber.

One-line bio

Matthew Haber is the owner of Real Sparks, an independent studio in Norwell, Massachusetts that makes small, calm browser games.

Short bio

Matthew Haber is the owner of Real Sparks, a design, marketing, and consulting studio founded in 2011 and based in Norwell, Massachusetts. Alongside websites and email for small businesses, Real Sparks designs and publishes small, calm browser games, including the word game Twinnow, the bubble-pop game Popeka!, and the tile puzzle Thrink. Matthew has worked in website design and marketing for 30 years.

Full bio

Matthew Haber is the owner of Real Sparks and has worked in website design and marketing for 30 years. Before and alongside Real Sparks, his career ran through luxury real estate marketing, digital consulting, marketing technology, and automotive software, including serving as Executive Vice President for Luxury Real Estate and as Director of Interactive Marketing for a large regional brokerage. Matthew worked at Deloitte Digital in Seattle, partnering with Fortune 500 and enterprise clients on digital strategy and customer experience. His engagements there included work for AT&T, Toyota, and Apple. In his day job, Matthew is a Principal Marketing Technologist at Zillow, where he helps define the strategy behind one of the industry's largest marketing technology ecosystems. Real Sparks is based in Norwell, Massachusetts.

The games

  • Popeka!: a relaxing bubble-pop game with 500 levels. Also on itch.io.
  • Thrink: a quick, brain-teasing tile puzzle about setting traps and springing chain reactions. Also on itch.io.
  • Twinnow: the word game where duplicates die; only unique words score. Also on itch.io.
  • Scribble Siege: a hand-drawn paper flick game of Space, Knights, and Tanks battles.
  • Breadcrumb: a calm daily link game, in as few steps as you can, with no timer.
  • Xenofront: a turn-based strategy game of hex warfare across alien worlds.
  • Creature Park: a learning adventure game for kids.
